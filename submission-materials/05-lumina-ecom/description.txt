<h2>Product Description</h2>

<p><strong>Lumina E-Com</strong> is a stylish, conversion-focused landing page template designed for e-commerce products, physical goods, consumer electronics, fashion items, and online stores. This template combines visual appeal with shopping psychology to create high-converting product pages that turn browsers into buyers.</p>

<h3>Key Sections</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>Hero Section</strong> - Stunning product showcase with compelling headline and CTA</li>
<li><strong>Features Section</strong> - Highlight key product features and benefits</li>
<li><strong>Product Showcase</strong> - Visual display of product from multiple angles</li>
<li><strong>Specifications Section</strong> - Technical details and product specs in easy-to-read format</li>
<li><strong>Reviews Section</strong> - Customer testimonials and ratings for social proof</li>
<li><strong>Comparison Table</strong> - Side-by-side comparison with alternatives or competitors</li>
<li><strong>What's In The Box</strong> - Show exactly what customers receive</li>
<li><strong>FAQ Section</strong> - Address common pre-purchase questions</li>
<li><strong>Corporate/Support Section</strong> - Trust-building information about your company</li>
<li><strong>CTA Section</strong> - Final push to complete the purchase</li>
</ul>
