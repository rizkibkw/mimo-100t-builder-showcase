FAQ

Common questions

What are the rate limits on the free plan?

The free plan includes 1,000 API calls per month, with a burst limit of 60 requests per minute. Pro plans get 1M calls/month with 1,000 requests/minute. Team plans are unlimited with custom rate limits based on your agreement.

Which programming languages are supported for SDK generation?

DevPort generates first-class SDKs for TypeScript, Python, Go, Ruby, Java, PHP, Swift, and Kotlin. All SDKs are generated from your OpenAPI 3.0 specification and include full type safety, comprehensive JSDoc/docstrings, and are version-controlled alongside your API.

How do I migrate an existing API to DevPort?

Migration is straightforward — just upload your existing OpenAPI spec (JSON or YAML), and DevPort will automatically generate SDKs, documentation, and set up monitoring. For REST APIs without a spec, our CLI can introspect your endpoints and generate one automatically. Most teams are fully migrated within a day.

Do you offer enterprise plans with custom SLAs?

Yes. Enterprise plans include 99.99% uptime SLA, dedicated infrastructure, custom rate limits, SSO/SAML authentication, audit logs, HIPAA/ SOC2 compliance support, and a dedicated customer success manager. Contact our sales team for pricing and a custom proposal.

What's your uptime guarantee and compensation policy?

DevPort guarantees 99.97% monthly uptime for Pro and Team plans. If we fall below this threshold in a calendar month, you'll receive a service credit proportional to the downtime. Enterprise customers receive enhanced SLAs with direct escalation paths and incident response guarantees.
