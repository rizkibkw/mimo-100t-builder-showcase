Quick Start Guide

Frequently asked questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How long does the battery last with ANC enabled?
With Active Noise Cancellation enabled, Lumina delivers up to 40 hours of continuous playback. Turn ANC off and you get up to 50 hours.
What does the 2-year warranty cover?
Our warranty covers manufacturing defects, driver failure, and hardware issues. It doesn't cover physical damage from drops or water damage beyond IPX4 rating. We also offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee.
How do returns work if I'm not satisfied?
Simply contact our support team within 30 days of delivery. We'll send you a prepaid return label. Once we receive the headphones in original condition, full refund is processed within 3-5 business days.
Are these compatible with my devices?
Lumina works with any Bluetooth-enabled device — phones, tablets, laptops, gaming consoles, and more. For wired connection, use the included 3.5mm cable or any standard audio cable. aptX Adaptive ensures optimal audio on Android devices.
Can I buy replacement ear cushions and other accessories?
Yes! Replacement ear cushions, additional cables, and a flight adapter are available in our online store. All accessories come with a 1-year warranty and free shipping on orders over $50.
