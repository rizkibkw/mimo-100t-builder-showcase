Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Vaultex

Each asset has a unique collateral factor ranging from 0% to 85%. This determines how much you can borrow against that asset. For example, ETH has a 80% collateral factor, meaning you can borrow up to $800 worth of other assets for every $1,000 of ETH supplied. Higher collateral factors allow for more borrowing power.
When your borrow balance exceeds your borrowing power due to asset price changes, your collateral becomes eligible for liquidation. Liquidators can then repay a portion of your debt in exchange for a bonus on your collateral. This mechanism maintains protocol solvency and ensures lenders can always withdraw their funds.
VTX token holders can propose and vote on protocol changes including interest rate models, new asset listings, risk parameters, and treasury allocations. Proposals pass with a quorum of 4% of total voting power and a majority vote. Changes are implemented automatically via timelock, ensuring transparency and community control.
Vaultex is not an AMM, so you don't provide liquidity to trading pools. Instead, you supply assets directly to lending markets. This means there's no impermanent loss risk—your supplied assets maintain their exact balance while earning interest. Your principal is always protected while you earn variable yields on your deposits.
Vaultex has been audited by Certik and our code is fully open source for community review. We maintain a $5M bug bounty program and have implemented comprehensive insurance through Nexus Mutual for added protection. While no system is 100% risk-free, we've built multiple layers of security including circuit breakers, rate limiting, and emergency pause functionality.
