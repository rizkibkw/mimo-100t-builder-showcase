FAQ

Questions people
usually ask

Can I import from Notion or Confluence?
Yes, one-click importers for Notion, Confluence, Evernote, Google Docs, Coda, and more. Everything transfers — documents, links, images, comments, and permissions. Most teams are fully migrated in under an hour.
How does the AI work? Is my data used for training?
Your data is never used to train our AI models. Period. All AI processing happens on your data within your private workspace. We use enterprise-grade encryption (AES-256) in transit and at rest. Enterprise customers also get dedicated AI infrastructure.
What's the difference between the Free and Pro plans?
The free plan includes 500 AI credits/month, 50 documents, and 3 workspaces — great for personal use. Pro gives you unlimited AI credits, unlimited documents, unlimited workspaces, priority support, and advanced AI features like auto-tagging and predictive insights.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, cancel with one click from your account settings. No cancellation fees, no awkward calls. You keep access until the end of your billing period, and your data is exportable anytime in JSON or Markdown format.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or education?
Yes! Nonprofits get 50% off Pro plans, and educational institutions get special site licenses. Email our team at education@nexus.app with your organization details and we'll set you up within 24 hours.
