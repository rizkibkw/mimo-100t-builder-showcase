FAQ

Common questions

Can I migrate from Notion or Evernote?
Yes! We have one-click importers for Notion, Evernote, Google Docs, Confluence, and more. Your notes, formatting, links, and attachments all transfer perfectly.
Is my data encrypted?
Absolutely. All your data is encrypted end-to-end. That means even we can't read your notes. We also offer SOC 2 Type II compliance for enterprise teams.
What's included in the free plan?
The free plan includes up to 100 notes, basic search, and single-device sync. No credit card required and it never expires.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, cancel with one click from your settings. No cancellation fees. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of your billing period.
